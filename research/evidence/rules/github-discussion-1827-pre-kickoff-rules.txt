----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hello,

We appreciate the enthusiasm of participants, but we ask that teams refrain from starting on their projects
until the start of the official November 14-15 hackathon (Saturday, 9am local time of your Local Event; if
Universal Event, start time is Saturday, 9am of the Team Owner's area of residence).

Working ahead of the hackathon can lead to disqualification.

However, before the hackathon starts, you may select a challenge to work on, review the resources, research
the topic, and form your team based on the skills you think are needed.

We encourage all participants to utilize the full 2 days (November 14-15) to build their projects - working
until the deadline.

In order to be eligible for Global Awards, you must use at least one NASA data source that is included with
the Resources of each challenge. Beyond that, all other NASA data and Space Agency Partner data included in
the Resources is strongly encouraged, but optional to use. All sources, including the NASA data, must be
cited as a reference in the project submission.

When you submit your project, you must list all of the data, resources, and tools that you used in your
project. Resources should include any code, text, and images (even if they are open source or freely
available) that you used when creating your project. If you are using any copyrighted materials, make sure
you have permission to use them.

Third party materials are allowed as long as you follow the above guidelines. If you are interested in
Global Awards, you must use at least one NASA resource in your project. The data is available in the
Resources tab of each Challenge: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/

Thank you.
